# Foundations & Grip: The Interfás of Energy (Nền tảng & Cách cầm vợt: Điểm tiếp xúc năng lượng)
This deep dive establishes the fundamental interfás between you, your vợt, and the bóng. At 50+, our priority isn't just power—it's structural integrity. A cách cầm vợt is not just how you hold the handle; it is how you connect your kilướiic chain to the bóng.
## Chapter 1: The Grip Philosophy (Triết lý Cầm vợt)
The cách cầm vợt is your primary "signal" to your brain. If the cách cầm vợt is rigid, your brain detects danger (tightness) and restricts movement to protect the joint. If the cách cầm vợt is adaptive, your nervous system remains fluid, allowing for the "whip" effect.
| |
| --- |
| Cầm vợt là tạo điểm kết nối, không phải là cầm nắm. |
| Áp lực 3/10 khi vung; 9/10 khi chạm bóng. |
| Dùng các cạnh (bevels) để định hình cú đánh. |
### The Three Master Grips (Ba Cách Cầm Vợt Chính)
* Continental (Bevel 2): The "Universal" cách cầm vợt. Used for Vôlei, Slice, and Phát Bóng. It is the most neutral interfás.
* Eastern/Semi-Western (Bevel 3-4): The "Cú Thuận Tay" chuẩn for 3.5 players. Offers a stable fás at contact.
* Western (Bevel 5+): Extreme spin. Requires higher physical demand; often risky for 50+ wrists.

## Chapter 2: Structural Integrity (Sự vững chãi của cấu trúc)
The "Tensegrity" of your forearm depends on how your knuckles and heel pad align with the vợt's bevels. Misalignment forces your small wrist muscles to compensate, leading to chronic strain.

| Cảm nhận trọng lượng trên khớp ngón tay. |
| Tránh "cầm chết" để bảo vệ khuỷu tay. |

## Chapter 3: The Contact Point Architecture (Cấu trúc Điểm tiếp xúc)
The cách cầm vợt dictates where your "ideal" contact điểm is. If you use a Western cách cầm vợt but hit the bóng in front of you like an Eastern player, you will shank it.
* Eastern/Semi-Western: Contact is in front and slightly to the side.
* Continental: Contact is out in front, allowing for the "open fás" carving motion of a cắt or Vôlei.
---
## Chapter 4: Practice Drills & Mental Cues (Bài tập & Khẩu quyết)
1. The "Check-Bevel" Drill: Before every điểm, look at your cách cầm vợt. Ensure the knuckle aligns with the bevel.
2. The "Soft-Hand" Drill: Hold the vợt with only your thumb and index finger. Swing gently. This forces you to feel the vợt's weight without squeezing.

| Scenario | Grip | Contact Point | Mental Cue |
