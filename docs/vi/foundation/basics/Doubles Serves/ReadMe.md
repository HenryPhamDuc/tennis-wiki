### Ý Tưởng Chính: "The Phát Bóng is a setup, not a weapon"
In singles, the Phát Bóng is a weapon. In doubles, the Phát Bóng is almost never a weapon — it's a setup. The 3.5 player who tries to "blast" the Phát Bóng in doubles loses more điểms than they win.
The smart doubles Phát Bóng is cắtd, plásd, and coordinated with the poacher.
The 4 Phát Bóng patterns map to 4 different doubles situations:
| # | Pattern | English | Tiếng Việt | 50+ delỗi? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phát Bóng Wide | Slice to Cú Trái Tay corner, pulls returner off court | Slice vào góc Cú Trái Tay, kéo người trả rộng | ✅ YES (60% of first phát bóngs) |
| 2 | Phát Bóng T | Down the middle, forces return UP at poacher | Xuôi giữa, ép return LÊN vào người cắt | Smart option (30% of first phát bóngs) |
| 3 | Phát Bóng Body | At the returner's torso, jams them | Vào thân người trả, ép họ | Underused weapon (10% of first phát bóngs) |
| 4 | Phát Bóng + Vôlei | You Phát Bóng AND follow to the lưới | Bạn giao VÀ theo lên lưới | For the brave (once or twice per set) |

### Key 3.5-level choices I made
- Made the Phát Bóng Wide lựa chọn mặc định cho 3.5 (60% of first phát bóngs). Slice to Cú Trái Tay corner pulls the returner wide, the poach covers the middle, the geometry favors the team.
At 50+, the cắt is the joint-friendly delỗi.
- Made the Phát Bóng T the "smart" option (30% of first phát bóngs). Down the middle forces the return UP, the poach hits DOWN, the poacher's dream. The cắt T is the 50+ smart delỗi.
- Made the Phát Bóng Body the "underused weapon" (10% of first phát bóngs). The body Phát Bóng is devastating at 3.5 but technically harder (tight margin).
Lead with Wide and T first, add Body once automatic.
- Made the Phát Bóng + Vôlei "for the brave" (once or twice per set). The 70% sprint to the lưới loads the knees and the heart. At 50+, this is a special-occasion pattern, not a delỗi.
- Translated the 4 Phát Bóng patterns into a frequency rule: 60% Wide, 30% T, 10% Body, +Vôlei rarely. The 80/20 cắt delỗi is layered on top — 80% of those 4 patterns are cắt, 20% are flat or kick.
- Added the "toss is the steering wheel" cue. Toss direction controls Phát Bóng direction. Toss right → cắt wide. Toss center → flat T. Toss left → kick or cắt T.
A 3.5 player who can't control the toss direction can't control the Phát Bóng direction.
- Added the "hand signal before Phát Bóng" coordination. The 50+ player with hearing loss can't hear "Poach!" — but a fist behind the back is silent, visible, and 50+ reliable.
- Tied the 4 Phát Bóng patterns to the existing 4 return patterns (Doubles Patterns deep dive). Together they form a compléte doubles playbook: 4 phát bóngs + 4 returns = 16 starting positions.
- Phrased the longevity framing: *"The cắt Phát Bóng is the 50+ doubles insurance policy.
A 50+ player who phát bóngs 80% cắt, 20% flat/kick, with plásment and poacher coordination will win 50% of their service váns.
The body that doesn't load the rotator cuff on every Phát Bóng is the body that plays for 20 more years."*
