# 🎾 Foundation - Tennis Fundamentals

Welcome to the Foundation section of the Tennis Knowledge Base. This is where you build the essential skills that every successful tennis player needs.

## 📚 What You'll Find Here

The Foundation section is organized into two main areas:

### [Basics](basics/)
Core fundamentals that every player must master:
- **Grips**: Continental, Eastern, Semi-Western - when and why to use each
- **Footwork**: Movement patterns, split steps, and recovery
- **Strokes**: Forehand, backhand, serve, and volley mechanics
- **Doubles**: Serves, patterns, and tactical positioning
- **Grip Changes**: The map to seamless transitions

### [Deep Dives](deep-dives/)
Extended explorations of foundational concepts:
- Detailed technique breakdowns
- Common mistakes and corrections
- Progression drills for each skill
- Video illustrations and coaching points

## 🎯 Who This Is For

This section is designed for the **3.5-4.5 player** who wants to:
- Solidify fundamental technique
- Understand the "why" behind each stroke
- Build a reliable, repeatable game
- Transition from recreational to competitive play

## 🚀 Get Started

Start with [Basics](basics/) if you're building your foundation from the ground up, or jump into [Deep Dives](deep-dives/) to explore specific topics in greater detail.
