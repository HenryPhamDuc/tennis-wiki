## Chapter 1 — The Big Idea: The Vertical Game

> 🧠 **Mental model
> Imagine a chess board. Horizontal moves (left-right) gain space. Vertical moves (forward-back, up-down) **change the rules of the game**.
A lob doesn't just put the ball in the corner — it tells the opponent: *you must hit the next shot from below your shoelaces, with your head looking up, while standing still or moving backward.* That'
s a fundamentally different problem from "return a flat ball while balanced at net." That's why lobs and overheads decide points.
>
> *Hình dung bàn cờ vua. Nước đi ngang (trái-phải) giành không gian. Nước đi dọc (trước-sau, lên-xuống) **thay đổi luật chơi**.
Lob không chỉ đưa bóng vào góc — nó nói với đối thủ: *anh phải đánh cú tiếp theo từ dưới dây giày, đầu ngước lên, trong khi đứng yên hoặc lùi lại*.
Đó là vấn đề căn bản khác với "trả bóng flat khi thăng bằng ở lưới." Đó là lý do lob và smash quyết định điểm.*

### The "Ladder of 4 Situations" — When to Use Vertical Tennis

Most 3.5 players think of the lob and the overhead as separate strokes. They're not — they're **paired answers to the same problem**: the ball is high (or the opponent is at the net).
Here is the entire decision tree:

| Situation |  | The shot | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
> ⚠️ **The 3.5 trap
> Most 3.5 players only ever practice Situation 4's lob volley (defensive) and never practice Situation 1 (defensive lob) or Situation 3 (overhead). They panic on every high ball.
They hit a weak "moon" lob when they should be hitting a deep slice lob. They flail at overheads.
**The whole point of this deep dive: practice all 4 situations, starting with the defensive lob and the controlled overhead.**
>
> *Hầu hết người 3.5 chỉ tập lob volley (phòng thủ) của Tình huống 4 và không bao giờ tập Tình huống 1 (lob phòng thủ) hoặc Tình huống 3 (overhead). Họ hoảng với mỗi bóng cao.
Họ đánh lob "trăng" yếu khi nên đánh lob slice sâu. Họ quơ overhead. **Toàn bộ ý nghĩa của deep dive này: tập cả 4 tình huống, bắt đầu với lob phòng thủ và overhead kiểm soát.***

### The Vertical Game in Tai Chi

| Principle | Vietnamese | What it means for lobs and overheads |
|---|---|---|
| *Hư Thực* (虛實) | Hư - Thực | A lob creates **hư** (emptiness) behind the opponent. An overhead exploits a real **thực** (substantial) high ball. Together they form the hư-thực cycle: you create space, then you fill it. |
| *Cung Bộ* (弓步) | Bước cung | The overhead stance is a literal bow stance — front foot forward, back foot anchored, weight loaded. Same as tai chi's *Cung Bộ*. |
| *Tỉnh Kình* (靜勁) | Lực tĩnh | A good lob is **quiet power** — minimum racket speed, maximum arc. A good overhead is the same — minimum effort, maximum placement. Neither shot is a "swing hard" shot. |
| *Lã Kình* (捋勁) | Cuộn lại | The defensive lob is a *Lã Kình* move — you yield to your opponent's pressure and redirect it upward and over their head. |

> 🩺 **The 50+ framing
> The lob and the overhead are the two most **longevity-friendly** shots in tennis. The lob is a slow, low-impact motion (no shoulder load, no violent rotation).
The overhead uses gravity as the engine — you don't need to swing hard; you just need to point the racket at the ball.
**Both shots save your joints compared to a baseline rally.** That's why we'll lead with them in this deep dive.
>
> *Lob và overhead là hai cú **thân thiện với tuổi tác** nhất trong tennis. Lob là động tác chậm, ít va chạm (không tải vai, không xoay bạo lực).
Overhead dùng trọng lực làm động cơ — anh không cần vung mạnh; chỉ cần chỉ mặt vợt vào bóng.
**Cả hai cú đều bảo vệ khớp so với rally baseline.** Đó là lý do ta sẽ dẫn đầu với chúng trong deep dive này.*

## Chapter 2 — The Lob: Defensive + Offensive (The Insurance Policy)

### The Two Lobs — Pick Yours (Lead with the Defensive Slice)

| Lob type |  | Vietnamese | When | Grip | Trajectory |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
> 🧠 **Which is for 3.5?
> **Lead with the defensive slice lob.** The offensive topspin lob is beautiful when Federer does it, but at 3.5 it requires: (1) reading the opponent's depth, (2) loading the legs, (3) generating rea
l racket head speed, (4) hitting a precise margin. Get all four right or the ball lands in the bottom of the net. **The defensive slice lob asks: (1) Continental grip, (2) high finish, (3) aim deep.
That's it.** The defensive lob is your insurance policy. The offensive lob is "for the brave."
>
> *Dẫn đầu với lob slice phòng thủ. Lob topspin tấn công đẹp khi Federer làm, nhưng ở 3.5 nó đòi hỏi: (1) đọc độ sâu đối thủ, (2) nạp chân, (3) tạo tốc độ đầu vợt thật, (4) biên độ chính xác.
Làm đúng cả bốn hoặc bóng rơi đáy lưới. **Lob slice phòng thủ đòi hỏi: (1) Continental, (2) kết thúc cao, (3) nhắm sâu. Chỉ vậy.** Lob phòng thủ là chính sách bảo hiểm.
Lob tấn công là "dành cho người dũng cảm."*

### Defensive Slice Lob — The Default

#### 🎯 Part 1 — The Setup (Grip + Stance + Backswing)

> 💡 **The "scratch your back" cue
> Stand in your ready position. Lift the racket as if to scratch the middle of your back. That's the lob backswing. **The higher the backswing, the higher the arc.** This is a good thing.
Most 3.5 players hit a "moon" lob because they keep the backswing low and try to lift with the wrist. Don't lift with the wrist. Lift with the backswing.
>
> *Đứng ở vị trí sẵn sàng. Nâng vợt như sắp gãi giữa lưng. Đó là backswing lob. **Backswing càng cao, vòng cung càng cao.** Đây là điều tốt.
Hầu hết người 3.5 đánh lob "trăng" vì giữ backswing thấp và cố nâng bằng cổ tay. Đừng nâng bằng cổ tay. Nâng bằng backswing.*

#### 🎯 Part 2 — The Contact (The "Up and Over" Feeling)

> 🎯 **The mirror check
> Stand in your open stance. Do the "scratch your back" backswing. Now imagine the ball is at chest height, in front of you.
Sweep the racket from low (knee) to high (head), face slightly open, shoulder shrug at the top. **Your racket should end ABOVE your head, almost like a mini-serve.** That's the lob.
Practice the air swing 10 times. Then add a ball.
>
> *Đứng ở tư thế mở. Làm backswing "gãi lưng." Giờ hình dung bóng ở tầm ngực, phía trước. Quét vợt từ thấp (gối) lên cao (đầu), mặt hơi mở, nhún vai ở đỉnh.
**Vợt nên kết thúc TRÊN đầu, gần như mini-serve.** Đó là lob. Tập vung không bóng 10 lần. Rồi thêm bóng.*

#### 🎯 Part 3 — The Aim and Depth (Where to Land It)

> 🧠 **The "Pass the test" mental model
> Before you hit the lob, ask: *will this ball clear the highest part of their reach AND land within 3 feet of the baseline?* If the answer to both is yes, hit it.
If either is no, either add more arc (lift the shoulder more) or aim for the deeper part of the court. **A lob that doesn't pass the test is a free point for the opponent.**
>
> *Trước khi đánh lob, hỏi: *bóng này có vượt phần cao nhất tầm với của họ VÀ rơi trong 90 cm từ baseline không?* Nếu cả hai câu trả lời là có, đánh.
Nếu một câu là không, hoặc thêm vòng cung (nhún vai hơn) hoặc nhắm phần sâu hơn của sân. **Lob không qua bài kiểm tra là điểm miễn phí cho đối thủ.***

### Offensive Topspin Lob — The Brave Shot (Reference)

> ⚠️ **The 3.5 trap with the offensive lob
> Most 3.5 players try to hit the offensive lob with the Continental grip. **That makes a slice lob, not a topspin lob.** With Continental, you can't generate the brushing motion needed for topspin.
If you're going to attempt the offensive lob, **you must use your groundstroke grip** (Semi-W or Eastern).
And you must swing **faster** than the defensive lob — the offensive lob is a real groundstroke that happens to go up.
>
> *Hầu hết người 3.5 cố đánh lob tấn công với Continental. **Đó tạo lob slice, không phải lob topspin.** Với Continental, anh không thể tạo động tác quét cần cho topspin.
Nếu định thử lob tấn công, **anh phải dùng grip groundstroke** (Semi-W hoặc Eastern). Và phải vung **nhanh hơn** lob phòng thủ — lob tấn công là groundstroke thật đi lên.*

### The Lob Decision Tree

```
YOU ARE AT THE BASELINE → Are you under pressure?

  ├─ YES, opponent is at the net and pushing me back
  │   └─ DEFENSIVE SLICE LOB (Continental, high backswing, deep aim)
  │
  ├─ NO, I have time and the ball is short
  │   └─ ATTACK — hit a normal groundstroke OR approach the net
  │
  ├─ MAYBE, opponent is at the baseline and hitting loopy
  │   └─ OFFENSIVE TOPSPIN LOB (groundstroke grip, brushing motion)
  │      ← Reference / For the brave
  │
  └─ I don't know what to do
      └─ DEFENSIVE SLICE LOB. Always. You cannot lose a point you successfully lob.
```

> 🩺 **The 50+ rule for lobs
> **At 50+, the lob is your best friend because it is the lowest-stress high-percentage shot in tennis.** No shoulder load, no violent rotation, no sprint, no sudden stopping.
Just: grip, backswing, lift, aim deep. **Practice the defensive slice lob for 2 months before attempting the offensive lob.** Your shoulder and your match record will thank you.
>
> *Ở tuổi 50+, lob là bạn tốt nhất vì nó là cú tỷ lệ thành công cao ít-căng-thẳng nhất trong tennis. Không tải vai, không xoay bạo lực, không chạy nước rút, không dừng đột ngột.
Chỉ: grip, backswing, nâng, nhắm sâu. **Tập lob slice phòng thủ 2 tháng trước khi thử lob tấn công.** Vai anh và thành tích trận đấu sẽ cảm ơn anh.*

## Chapter 3 — The Overhead: The Sane Smash (The 50+ Reward)

### Why Recreational Players Miss Overheads

> 🧠 **The "serve that already happened" mental model
> Every overhead is a serve. The opponent just tossed it for you. You have **all the time in the world** to set up. The ball is in the air, coming down at a comfortable pace.
The net is the same height. The court is the same shape. The only difference from your serve: you're not tossing, you're intercepting.
**Treat the overhead like a serve and your success rate will jump from 30% to 80%.**
>
> *Mỗi overhead là một cú giao bóng. Đối thủ vừa tung giùm anh. Anh có **tất cả thời gian thế giới** để setup. Bóng ở trên không, rơi xuống tốc độ thoải mái. Lưới cùng cao. Sân cùng hình.
Khác biệt duy nhất với serve: anh không tung, anh chặn. **Đối xử overhead như serve và tỷ lệ thành công sẽ nhảy từ 30% lên 80%.***

### The Sane Smash — 3.5+ Overhead Framework

#### 🎯 Part 1 — The Tracking and Stance

> 💡 **The "two-arm" cue
> At the trophy position of the overhead, your arms should look **identical** to the trophy position of your serve.
Non-dominant arm pointing at the ball (or just tossed), racket arm bent with head above. If you've ever hit a serve, you already know this position. The overhead is just the next rep.
>
> *Ở vị trí trophy của overhead, hai tay anh nên trông **giống hệt** vị trí trophy của serve. Tay không thuận chỉ về bóng (hoặc vừa tung), tay vợt gập với đầu vợt trên.
Nếu anh đã từng giao bóng, anh đã biết vị trí này. Overhead chỉ là rep tiếp theo.*

#### 🎯 Part 2 — The Contact and Finish (The "Fall, Don't Swing")

> 🧠 **The "downward" mental model
> Watch a pro hit an overhead. The racket head is at the top, the ball is below it, the racket "falls" through the ball. The ball is in the air for less than half a second.
**You don't have time to swing.** You have time to point and let go. The "swing" is just the racket continuing its downward path after the ball has been hit. **Less is more.**
>
> *Xem pro đánh overhead. Đầu vợt ở trên, bóng ở dưới, vợt "rơi" xuyên qua bóng. Bóng ở trên không chưa đến nửa giây. **Anh không có thời gian vung.** Anh có thời gian chỉ và buông.
"Vung" chỉ là vợt tiếp tục đường đi xuống sau khi bóng bị đánh. **Ít hơn là nhiều hơn.***

> ⚠️ **The 3.5 trap with overheads
> Recreational players often try to "muscle" the overhead. They grip it tight, swing hard, and the ball flies long or into the net.
**The Sane Smash is the opposite: grip pressure 3-4/10, swing speed 30% of a forehand, and the ball goes IN because gravity and downward angle do the work.** A controlled 50 mph overhead is a winner a
t 3.5. A panicked 80 mph overhead is a fault.
>
> *Người nghiệp dư thường cố "vung" overhead. Họ nắm chặt, vung mạnh, và bóng bay dài hoặc vào lưới.
**Smash kiểm soát là ngược lại: áp lực grip 3-4/10, tốc độ vung 30% forehand, và bóng vào sân vì trọng lực và góc đi xuống làm việc.** Overhead 50 mph kiểm soát là winner ở 3.5.
Overhead 80 mph hoảng loạn là lỗi.*

### The Three Overhead Situations

| Situation | Position of the ball | The Sane Smash adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Easy high ball** (the opponent's lob is short) | Ball is at or above net height, falling comfortably | **Standard smash.** Full extension, full Sane Smash. Aim cross-court, place the ball. |
| **2. Behind you** (the opponent's lob is deep) | Ball is behind your body, above your head | **Turn and shuffle.** Side-shuffle back so the ball is in front of you. Don't try to hit an overhead with the ball behind you — that's a backhand smash, and at 3.5, the only backhand smash worth hitting is a controlled block back. **Easier solution:** if the lob is too deep, let it bounce. You now have a normal groundstroke. |
| **3. Moving backward** (you're running back as the lob drops) | Ball is above you, you're still moving | **Plant, then hit.** Stop your feet first, then hit. Hitting on the run is a recipe for error. The Sane Smash is a still shot, not a moving shot. |

> 🩺 **The 50+ rule for overheads
> **At 50+, the Sane Smash is your reward for hitting good defensive lobs in your previous points.** The ball comes to you, gravity does the work, your shoulder doesn't take a heavy load.
The only risk is the **head-still cue** — your neck doesn't love craning backward.
**Keep your chin level, eyes tracking the ball, and let the ball come DOWN to you instead of you reaching UP for it.** If the lob is so high that you have to crane your neck, **let it bounce.** A 3.5-
controlled forehand from the baseline is a better shot than a 50+ overstretched overhead.
>
> *Ở tuổi 50+, Smash Kiểm Soát là phần thưởng cho việc đánh lob phòng thủ tốt ở các điểm trước. Bóng tới chỗ anh, trọng lực làm việc, vai anh không chịu tải nặng.
Rủi ro duy nhất là **tín hiệu đầu-yên** — cổ anh không thích ngửa sau.
**Giữ cằm ngang, mắt theo bóng, và để bóng ĐẾN chỗ anh thay vì anh vươn LÊN cho nó.** Nếu lob quá cao đến mức phải vặn cổ, **hãy để nó nảy.** Forehand 3.5 kiểm soát từ baseline là cú tốt hơn overhead 
50+ vươn quá.*

## Chapter 4 — The Specialty Shots: Lob Volley + Counter-Overhead

### 4A. The Lob Volley — Your Net Emergency Parachute

> 🧠 **The lob volley is to the net what the defensive lob is to the baseline.** Same idea, same aim, different body position.
If you've already practiced the defensive lob, the lob volley is just the same shot from 30 feet closer to the net.
>
> *Lob volley là với lưới những gì lob phòng thủ là với baseline. Cùng ý tưởng, cùng nhắm, vị trí cơ thể khác. Nếu anh đã tập lob phòng thủ, lob volley chỉ là cùng cú từ 9 mét gần lưới hơn.*

> 💡 **The "parachute" mental model
> A lob volley is not a winner. It's a **parachute** — it doesn't end the point, it gives you a soft landing when the situation is dangerous.
**Use the lob volley to escape a no-pass scenario, then re-attack on the next shot.** A lob volley followed by a smart net approach is a complete pattern.
>
> *Lob volley không phải cú winner. Nó là **dù** — nó không kết thúc điểm, nó cho anh hạ cánh mềm khi tình huống nguy hiểm.
**Dùng lob volley để thoát tình huống không pass được, rồi tấn công lại ở cú tiếp theo.** Lob volley theo sau bởi net approach thông minh là pattern hoàn chỉnh.*

### 4B. The Counter-Overhead — Your Net Finishing Move

> ⚠️ **The 3.5 trap with the counter-overhead
> Many 3.5 players see the lob, panic, and run straight back without turning their body. They arrive at the ball looking up, off-balance, and hit a weak floater.
**The fix: track the lob, side-shuffle back, then TURN into the serve stance.** The turn is what gives you the same Sane Smash as your serve.
>
> *Nhiều người 3.5 thấy lob, hoảng, và chạy thẳng lùi mà không xoay người. Họ tới chỗ bóng nhìn lên, mất thăng bằng, và đánh floater yếu.
**Sửa: theo lob, side-shuffle lùi, rồi XOAY vào tư thế serve.** Xoay là thứ cho anh cùng Smash Kiểm Soát như serve.*

### The Vertical Game Decision Tree

```
BALL IS IN THE AIR → What's the situation?

  ├─ I lobbed it, opponent is running back
  │   └─ Did they reach it?
  │       ├─ YES, they hit a defensive ball → RECOVER, hit a normal groundstroke
  │       └─ NO, it's a winner → CONGRATULATIONS
  │
  ├─ Opponent lobbed, I'm at the baseline
  │   └─ Can I get in position?
  │       ├─ YES → SANE SMASH (Ch 3): track, position, turn, fall
  │       └─ NO (lob too deep, I'm out of position) → LET IT BOUNCE, hit a normal forehand/backhand
  │
  ├─ Opponent lobbed, I'm at the net
  │   └─ Can I get in position?
  │       ├─ YES → COUNTER-OVERHEAD (Ch 4B): shuffle back, turn, Sane Smash
  │       └─ NO (lob is perfect, I'm stuck at net) → LOB VOLLEY (Ch 4A): high contact, deep aim, recover to net
  │
  └─ I'm at the baseline, opponent is at the net, I'm under pressure
      └─ DEFENSIVE LOB (Ch 2): Continental, high backswing, deep aim
```

> 🩺 **The 50+ pattern in doubles
> **The vertical game is the best doubles weapon for 50+ players.** Why? Doubles opponents at the net are usually 8-10 feet away from you.
A defensive lob is the easiest way to neutralize them — they have to hit a high overhead on the move. And your doubles partner can poach the weak reply.
**The pattern: my partner serves, I hit a deep defensive lob, partner poaches the reply. Three shots, one point.** This is the 50+ doubles cheat code.
>
> *Trò chơi dọc là vũ khí đôi tốt nhất cho người 50+. Tại sao? Đối thủ đánh đôi ở lưới thường cách anh 2,5-3 mét.
Lob phòng thủ là cách dễ nhất để vô hiệu hóa họ — họ phải đánh overhead cao trong khi di chuyển. Và bạn đôi của anh có thể vồ bóng trả lời yếu.
**Pattern: bạn tôi giao bóng, tôi đánh lob phòng thủ sâu, bạn vồ bóng trả lời. Ba cú, một điểm.** Đây là mã cheat đôi 50+.*

### 🧠 Master Cue List

| Cue | Signal | When | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Scratch your back"** | Racket head UP, elbow bent, like scratching your back with the racket | Setup, before the lob | Defensive lob |
| **"Shoulder shrug"** | Shrug the hitting shoulder UP toward the ear at contact | Contact | Defensive lob |
| **"Aim deep, into the corner"** | The lob should land within 3 feet of the baseline, near the sideline | Aim | All lobs |
| **"Continental, always"** | V-to-bevel-2 grip for every lob and every overhead | Grip | All lobs and overheads |
| **"Trophy position"** | Both arms up, like the start of a serve | Setup, before the overhead | Overhead smash |
| **"Fall, don't swing"** | The racket head falls down through the ball; gravity does the work | Contact | Overhead smash |
| **"PUT IT DOWN"** | Not "SMASH" — you are placing the ball, not generating pace | Mental | Overhead smash |
| **"Cross-court is king"** | Aim cross-court unless you have a clean down-the-line opportunity | Aim | Overhead smash |
| **"Head still, chin level"** | Don't tilt your head back to look at the ball | Setup | Overhead smash |
| **"Let it bounce if too deep"** | If the lob is too high or too far back, let it bounce and hit a groundstroke | Decision | Overhead smash |
| **"High contact, not low"** | The lob volley only works when the ball is at or above shoulder height | Setup | Lob volley |
| **"Recover to the net"** | After the lob volley, get back to the net for the next shot | Recovery | Lob volley |
| **"70/30"** | 70% of your overhead weight is on the BACK leg at contact | Stance | Overhead smash |
| **"Breathe out"** | Small exhale on contact to release tension | Pre-contact | All lobs and overheads |
| **"Survive, then shine"** | The lob and overhead are first about getting the ball back; placement is secondary | Mental | All lobs and overheads |

### ⚠️ The 7 Common Errors at 3.5

| Error | What it looks like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **"Flat lob"** | Backswing is low, swing is horizontal, ball flies low and gets smashed | Backswing UP, racket path low-to-high, shoulder shrug |
| **"Wrong grip on lob"** | Using Semi-Western or Eastern, lob flies long | Continental for the defensive lob, groundstroke grip ONLY for the offensive topspin lob |
| **"Short lob"** | Lob lands in the service box, opponent hits an easy overhead | Aim 3 feet short of the baseline, into the corner |
| **"Panicked overhead"** | Runs to the ball, swings hard, ball flies long or into the net | Sane Smash: track, position, turn, fall. Grip 3-4/10, swing 30% |
| **"Craning the neck"** | Head tilts back to look at the ball, body off-balance | Head still, eyes on the ball, chin level. Let the ball come DOWN to you |
| **"Hitting behind the body"** | Trying to smash a ball that's behind you, instead of letting it bounce | If the lob is too deep, LET IT BOUNCE. A 3.5 groundstroke is better than a 50+ overstretched smash |
| **"Lob volley from a low contact"** | Trying to lob volley a ball at the waist — goes into the net | Lob volley ONLY from high contact (shoulder height or above). If low, hit a normal volley or half-volley |

### 🩺 The 50+ Reality Check

| Issue | At 50+ the risk is... | The lob/overhead-friendly answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Shoulder strain on overhead** | Cranking the shoulder on a high ball, rotator cuff irritation | Sane Smash uses gravity, not muscle. Grip 3-4/10. Reach but don't crank. |
| **Neck strain on overhead** | Craning backward to look at a high ball | Head still, eyes tracking the ball. Let it come DOWN to you. If too high, let it bounce. |
| **Knee strain on lob recovery** | Sprinting back to position for the next shot after a lob | Use the "small step" recovery. Lob buys you time, use it to walk back, not sprint back. |
| **Back strain on overhead** | Hyperextending the lower back to reach a high ball | Bend the knees, not the back. Trophy position uses knee bend + hip hinge, not back arch. |
| **Wrist/elbow on the offensive lob** | Brushing too hard on the topspin lob | Skip the offensive lob entirely at 50+. The defensive slice lob does the same job with zero strain. |
| **Loss of confidence on high balls** | Fear of the overhead leads to weak, floating "moon" returns | Sane Smash drill (Drill 3) builds confidence. 10 Sane Smashes in a row = brain rewires. |
| **Playing horizontal tennis only** | Never using the vertical game, points decided by groundstroke exchanges | Lead with the defensive lob. Practice once per session. Within a month, lob will be a reflex. |

> 🩺 **The single most important 50+ rule for lobs and overheads
> **You are not trying to win the point with the lob or the overhead.
You are trying to NOT LOSE the point.** A defensive lob that lands deep, even if the opponent retrieves it, gives you time to recover.
A Sane Smash that lands in the correct service box, even if the opponent gets it back, puts you in attack position. **Survival first, placement second, pace last. Always.**
>
> *Anh không cố ghi điểm bằng lob hay overhead. Anh cố KHÔNG MẤT điểm. Lob phòng thủ rơi sâu, kể cả nếu đối thủ cứu được, cho anh thời gian hồi phục.
Smash Kiểm Soát rơi đúng service box, kể cả nếu đối thủ trả lại, đặt anh ở vị trí tấn công. **Sống sót trước, đặt bóng sau, lực cuối cùng. Luôn luôn.***
