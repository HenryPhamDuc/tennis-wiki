# 🧠 Advanced - Strategic Tennis Mastery

Welcome to the Advanced section, where you transform solid fundamentals into winning strategies. This is where good players become great.

## 📚 What You'll Find Here

The Advanced section builds on your foundation and adds:

### [Basics](basics/)
Advanced fundamentals for competitive play:
- **Shot Selection**: When to attack, defend, or neutralize
- **Pattern Recognition**: Reading opponents and anticipating shots
- **Court Positioning**: Strategic court coverage and recovery
- **Spin and Pace**: Using variety to disrupt rhythm
- **Match Management**: Energy conservation and momentum control

### [Deep Dives](deep-dives/)
In-depth explorations of advanced concepts:
- **Embodied Cognition**: Tennis as a living lab for mind-body connection
- **Two Engines**: Rotational vs. linear power generation
- **Proprioception**: Your hidden GPS for shot accuracy
- **Reflex Arcs**: The 3 speeds of your body's reactions
- **Tensegrity Body**: The skeleton that floats under load
- **Fascia Spirals**: The anatomy trains connection
- **X-Factor Anatomy**: The 45° pre-load for maximum rotation
- **Head Position & Vestibular**: The first balance decision

## 🎯 Who This Is For

This section is designed for the **4.0+ player** who wants to:
- Develop strategic thinking on court
- Master mental game and pressure situations
- Understand the biomechanics of elite players
- Add tactical weapons to their arsenal
- Compete effectively in tournaments

## 🚀 Get Started

Start with [Basics](basics/) to refine your advanced fundamentals, or explore [Deep Dives](deep-dives/) to understand the science behind elite performance.
